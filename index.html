Japa day starts at

Hour (0–12) at which the day rolls over. At 3, a session running from 11pm to 1am counts entirely to the day it began, instead of splitting across midnight.

Mark complete freezes the record and moves it below the active recitations in Progress. Archive hides it from Progress entirely and moves it to Archived at the bottom of Setup. Nothing is ever deleted — Reopen and Restore put things back exactly as they were.

Aura
Intensity

Colour and animation of the graha field behind the ring. Dim is easiest on the eyes at night.

Ripples

Rings travelling outward from the mala. Turn off to leave only the breath and the rays.

Each graha carries its own colour, breath rate and ray spacing, drawn from the complexions given in Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra 3.16–17. Saturn is black in the verse; indigo is used here because black cannot glow.

Counting without the screen
Any key counts

Bluetooth clicker, page-turner pedal or keyboard. Pair it, then press any key.

Ding when a mala completes

For when the phone is out of reach and you can't feel the buzz. Only sounds when the mala is finished with the button.

Screen tap counts

Turn off so a pocket or a knee cannot add beads.

Buzz on each bead

Longer buzz when a mala completes.

Keep screen awake

Stops the display sleeping mid-mala. Always on while counting a recitation. Low Power Mode or battery saver can still override it — the phone has the final say.

Phone volume keys cannot be read by a browser, so they will not count. A cheap Bluetooth camera shutter or a music page-turner pedal is the reliable physical button — most send a normal key press.
